Why take a 3 minute typing test?

Many job applications, data-entry roles and government or university typing tests use 3 to 5 minutes, because a short test can hide inconsistent typing. Three minutes shows your sustained speed — the pace you keep when the first burst of energy wears off. The speed chart at the end shows whether you slowed down.

Preparing for a typing test for a job

  • Practise with Sentences or Punctuation & numbers mode — real job tests use proper text with capitals and punctuation.
  • Employers usually ask for 35–45 WPM for general office work and 50–60+ WPM for typists and data entry, often with 90–95% accuracy.
  • Accuracy counts: a test that removes points for errors can make 60 WPM with mistakes score lower than 50 WPM without.
