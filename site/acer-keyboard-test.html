How to use the Acer Keyboard Test

  1. Tick Numeric keypad if your Acer has one
  2. Press every key, including the narrow number-pad keys
  3. If only brightness or volume keys fail, reinstall Acer Quick Access

Testing an Acer laptop keyboard

This page uses the Windows laptop layout found on Acer Aspire, Swift, Nitro and Predator laptops. Tick Numeric keypad if yours has one — on many 15.6″ Acers the number keys are narrower than the letters, so press them carefully.

Green means the key works, blue means it is held down, and dashed keys (Fn) can't be seen by any website. The full colour guide and the general step-by-step fixes for a dead key are on the laptop keyboard test page; this page covers what is specific to Acer.

Acer keys that behave differently

  • Function row. On many Acer models the top-row behaviour is a BIOS setting: restart, press F2, and on the Main tab look for Function key behavior or Hotkey Mode. Some newer models toggle it with Fn + Esc.
  • Hotkeys need Acer software. Brightness, volume and airplane-mode keys rely on Acer Quick Access (Launch Manager on older laptops). If the letters pass here but those keys do nothing, reinstall it from Acer's support page for your model.
  • Backlight. On many Aspire and Swift models it is Fn + F9 (Fn + F8 on some).
  • Nitro and Predator keys. The NitroSense, PredatorSense and Turbo keys open Acer's own apps and may not light up in a browser.
  • € and $ keys. Older Aspire keyboards have euro and dollar keys above the left and right arrows. They type the symbols but are not drawn as separate keys here.
  • Power key. On many Swift and Aspire models the power button is the top-right key. Skip it.

If keys fail on your Acer

Open Acer Care Center to check for driver and BIOS updates, then test again. If the same keys stay grey afterwards, connect a USB keyboard: if that one passes, the built-in keyboard or its ribbon cable is at fault and needs repair.

Frequently asked questions

Why don't my Acer brightness and volume keys work?

Acer hotkeys rely on the Acer Quick Access app (older models: Launch Manager). Reinstall it from the Acer support page for your model if the keys do nothing.

How do I turn on the keyboard backlight on an Acer?

On many Aspire and Swift models it is Fn + F9 (Fn + F8 on some). Nitro and Predator laptops also let you set the lighting in NitroSense or PredatorSense.

What are the € and $ keys next to the arrows on my Acer?

Older Aspire keyboards have dedicated euro and dollar keys above the left and right arrows. They type the currency symbols and may not light up as separate keys in a browser test.
