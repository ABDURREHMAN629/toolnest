About this tool

Set an online alarm clock that rings in your browser at the time you choose. Online Alarm Clock is part of WebToolNest's collection of free date & time. It works instantly in your browser with no account and no installation.

Online alarm clock with sound

Set an alarm time, give it a label (“Wake up”, “Take medicine”) and pick a sound — Classic, Beep, Bell or Digital. Add as many as you like: it's an online alarm clock multiple alarms tool, and each one can be switched on or off. When an alarm rings, choose Stop or Snooze 5 min. Your alarms are saved in this browser, and the page shows a big live clock and today's date. It's an online alarm clock free of sign-ups — an online alarm clock with sound that keeps ringing until you stop it.

Make sure it rings

  • Keep this tab open and your computer awake — the online alarm clock for computer rings in your browser, so a sleeping laptop can't ring.
  • Turn the volume up. For an online alarm clock loud enough — or an online alarm clock for heavy sleepers — choose Digital or Beep and use external speakers.
  • Allow notifications so you see a pop-up even in another tab.

Laptop, PC and classroom

It works as an online alarm clock for laptop and an online alarm clock for pc users at their desk, and an online alarm clock for students to mark the end of study blocks. Teachers use it as an online alarm clock for classroom and an online alarm clock for kids to signal tidy-up time. On a projector, the big clock doubles as an online digital clock for classroom and an online digital clock for exams — or an online digital clock for desktop on a spare screen.

Alarm or timer?

An alarm rings at a time of day; a timer rings after a duration. For an online alarm clock timer (“ring in 20 minutes”), use the Countdown Timer.
