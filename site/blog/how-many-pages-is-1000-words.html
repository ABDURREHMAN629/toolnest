How Many Pages Is 1,000 Words? (Single and Double Spaced Chart)

Quick answer: 1,000 words is about 2 pages single-spaced or 4 pages double-spaced, using 12 pt Times New Roman or Arial with 1-inch (2.5 cm) margins.

Teachers, editors and application forms often set limits in pages, while you write in words — or the other way round. The standard rule of thumb is 500 words per single-spaced page and 250 words per double-spaced page. Here's a full chart, plus the settings that change the count.

Going the other way: a 1-page essay is about 250–500 words, a 5-page essay about 1,250 words double-spaced, and a 10-page paper about 2,500 words double-spaced.

In Microsoft Word, the word count is in the status bar at the bottom of the window (or Review → Word Count). In Google Docs, press Ctrl + Shift + C (Cmd + Shift + C on a Mac). Both also show your page count.

What changes the number of words per page

  • Line spacing. Double spacing halves the words per page; 1.5 spacing gives about 330–350 words per page.
  • Font. Times New Roman is compact. Arial, Calibri and Verdana are wider, so slightly fewer words fit. Courier and other monospaced fonts fit noticeably fewer.
  • Font size. 11 pt fits more words than 12 pt; 14 pt fits far fewer.
  • Margins. Wider margins mean fewer words per page.
  • Paragraphs, headings and lists. Lots of short paragraphs, dialogue, headings or bullet points leave white space and reduce the count.
  • Paper size. A4 is slightly longer than US Letter, so it fits a few more lines per page.

Essay formats: MLA, APA and Chicago

MLA, APA and Chicago style all use double spacing, a readable 12 pt font and 1-inch margins, so the double-spaced column of the chart applies: about 250 words per page. A 1,000-word essay in MLA or APA format is about 4 pages, plus any title page and references page, which usually don't count toward the limit.

Handwritten pages and book pages

  • Handwritten: roughly 250–300 words per side of standard lined paper, so 1,000 words is about 3–4 handwritten pages. Large handwriting can double that.
  • Books: a typical paperback page holds about 250–300 words, so 1,000 words is 3–4 book pages. A 300-page novel is usually 75,000–90,000 words.

How long does 1,000 words take to read or say?

Reading silently, the average adult reads about 238 words per minute, so 1,000 words takes about 4 minutes. Read aloud at a typical speaking pace of 130 words per minute, it takes about 7½–8 minutes. For speeches, see how many words is a 5-minute speech, or use the converter:

Tips for hitting a page or word limit

  • Too long? Cut filler words ("very", "really", "in order to"), merge repetitive sentences and remove a weak example rather than shrinking the font.
  • Too short? Add evidence, an example or a counter-argument — not padding. Readers and graders notice fluff.
  • Don't fiddle with formatting. Teachers recognise 12.5 pt fonts and widened margins. Stick to the required format and adjust the writing.
  • Character limits on forms and applications count letters, not words — check them with the character counter.

Frequently asked questions

How many pages is 1,000 words double spaced?

About 4 pages, using 12 pt Times New Roman or Arial with 1-inch margins — the standard settings for MLA and APA essays.

How many pages is 1,000 words handwritten?

Roughly 3–4 pages on standard lined paper, but it depends a lot on the size of your handwriting.

How long does it take to read 1,000 words?

About 4 minutes reading silently at an average 238 words per minute, or about 7–8 minutes reading aloud.

How many words is a 5-page essay?

About 1,250 words double-spaced or 2,500 words single-spaced.

How many pages is 1,000 words in a book?

About 3–4 pages in a typical paperback, which fits around 250–300 words per page.
