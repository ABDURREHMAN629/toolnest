How Many Words Is a 5-Minute Speech? (Chart for 1 to 60 Minutes)

Quick answer: a 5-minute speech is about 650 words at an average speaking pace of 130 words per minute. Depending on how fast you talk, that ranges from about 500 words (slow, 100 wpm) to 800 words (fast, 160 wpm).

Whether you're writing a wedding toast, a class presentation or a conference talk, the question is the same: how much do I need to write to fill the time — without running over? The answer depends on one number: your speaking pace, measured in words per minute (wpm). Below is a chart you can use straight away, the typical lengths of common speeches, and a simple way to measure your own pace.

Speech length chart: minutes to words

Most people speak at roughly 120–150 words per minute when presenting, so 130 wpm is a good middle estimate. Slow, deliberate speakers sit near 100 wpm, and fast talkers reach 160 wpm or more.

How long is a speech of a given word count?

Going the other way, divide the number of words by your pace. At 130 words per minute:

  • 500 words ≈ 3 minutes 50 seconds
  • 700 words ≈ 5 minutes 25 seconds
  • 1,000 words ≈ 7 minutes 40 seconds (6¼ minutes if you're fast, 10 minutes if you're slow)
  • 1,500 words ≈ 11 minutes 30 seconds
  • 2,000 words ≈ 15 minutes 25 seconds

Reading silently is much faster than speaking. A large review of reading research (Brysbaert, 2019) put the average adult's silent reading speed for English non-fiction at about 238 words per minute — which is why a 1,000-word article takes only about four minutes to read but nearly eight to read aloud.

For events with a strict limit, such as a competition or a timed class assignment, aim for about 90% of the maximum. A 5-minute slot is safer with 580–600 words than with 650, because nerves, laughter and applause all eat into your time.

What changes your speaking pace

  • Pauses. Good speakers pause after key points. Planned pauses can easily add 10–15% to a speech.
  • Slides and demos. Every time you click to a new slide or show something, you stop talking for a moment.
  • Nerves. Most people speak faster on stage than in rehearsal — sometimes 20 words per minute faster.
  • Audience reactions. Laughter, applause or questions add time you can't fully plan for.
  • Language and content. Technical material, numbers and names slow you down. Speaking in a second language usually does too.

How to time your speech in 4 steps

  1. Count your words. Paste your draft into the Word Counter (it also shows an estimated speaking time).
  2. Measure your real pace. Start a stopwatch, read your speech aloud at your natural speed for exactly one minute, and count how many words you got through. That number is your words per minute.
  3. Calculate the length. Divide your total words by your pace — or enter both into the Words to Minutes Converter, which also works backwards to tell you how many words you need.
  4. Rehearse the whole thing once. A full timed run-through is the only way to include pauses and transitions. Cut or add a paragraph and run it again.

Tips for writing a speech that fits the time

  • Write for the ear. Short sentences are easier to say and easier to follow, and they keep your pace steady.
  • Mark optional sections. Highlight one paragraph you can drop if you're running long, and one story you can add if you finish early.
  • Put timings in your notes. Write the expected time next to each section (for example "2:00 — second story") so you can check your progress on stage.
  • Don't speed up to fit. If your speech is too long, cut words rather than talking faster — rushed speeches are hard to follow and sound nervous.

Frequently asked questions

How many words is a 3-minute speech?

About 390 words at an average pace of 130 words per minute — anywhere from 300 words if you speak slowly to 480 words if you speak fast.

How many words is a 10-minute speech?

About 1,300 words at 130 words per minute, or 1,000 to 1,600 words depending on how fast you speak.

How long is a 1,000-word speech?

About 7 minutes 40 seconds at an average pace of 130 words per minute. A fast speaker finishes in about 6 minutes 15 seconds; a slow one takes about 10 minutes.

How many pages is a 5-minute speech?

Around 650 words is roughly 1.3 pages single-spaced or 2.6 pages double-spaced, using the common estimate of 500 words per single-spaced page.

Is 150 words per minute too fast for a speech?

No. 150 words per minute is a normal conversational pace. For a formal presentation, 120–150 is comfortable for most audiences; much above 170 becomes hard to follow.
