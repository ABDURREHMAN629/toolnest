How to Remove the Background From an Image for Free

Removing a background used to mean careful work in Photoshop. Now AI does it in seconds — for product photos, profile pictures, passport photos, logos, stickers and presentations. Here's how to do it for free, plus built-in options on the devices and apps you already use.

How to remove a background for free (any device)

  1. Open Remove Background and choose a photo.
  2. The first time, the AI model downloads to your browser (it's then remembered). The background is removed automatically.
  3. Pick a new background if you want one: transparent, white, a colour of your choice, or a blurred version of the original.
  4. Click Download PNG.

Popular uses

  • Product photos for online stores: a clean white background is what marketplaces like Amazon and many shops expect for main product images.
  • Profile pictures: remove a messy background and add a colour or blur.
  • Passport and ID photos: choose a white background, then crop it with the passport photo maker.
  • Logos: turn a logo on a white box into a transparent PNG that sits cleanly on any colour.
  • Presentations and posters: cut-out people and objects look much more polished than rectangles.

Remove a background on iPhone, iPad and Mac

On iOS 16 or later, open a photo in the Photos app, touch and hold the main subject until a glowing outline appears, then tap Copy or Share. You can paste the cut-out into Messages, Notes or another app. On a Mac (macOS Ventura and later), right-click an image in Finder and choose Quick Actions → Remove Background.

Remove a background in PowerPoint and Word

Select the picture, go to Picture Format → Remove Background, adjust the areas to keep or remove, and click Keep Changes. It works best on simple images; right-click the picture and choose Save as Picture (PNG) to export the result.

Remove a background in Canva and Photoshop

  • Canva: select the image and choose Edit → BG Remover. It's part of Canva's paid plans, so on the free plan, remove the background here first and upload the PNG to Canva.
  • Photoshop: open the image and use Remove Background in the Properties panel's Quick Actions, then refine the mask if needed.
  • Google Slides: has no background removal, so remove it first and insert the transparent PNG.

Tips for clean edges

  • Contrast helps. A subject that stands out from the background — dark hair against a light wall, for example — gives the cleanest cut-out.
  • Use sharp photos. Blurry edges become fuzzy outlines.
  • Good light, no harsh shadows. Shadows on the ground may be kept or removed unpredictably.
  • Keep the PNG. JPG files can't be transparent — saving as JPG turns the empty area white.
  • Zoom in before you use it. Check the edges around hair, fingers and transparent objects like glasses.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a completely free background remover?

Yes. The background remover on WebToolNest is free with no sign-up and no watermark, and it gives you a full-resolution PNG. The photo is processed in your browser, not uploaded.

Why does my image still have a white background after removing it?

You probably saved it as a JPG. JPG files can't be transparent, so the empty area becomes white. Save as PNG to keep the transparency.

Can I remove the background on my iPhone?

Yes, on iOS 16 and later. Open the photo in Photos, touch and hold the subject until it lights up, then choose Copy or Share to use the cut-out subject without its background.

Does background removal work on hair?

Modern AI tools handle most hair well, especially against a plain, contrasting background. Very fine flyaway hair against a busy background is the hardest case.

How do I make a passport photo background white?

Remove the background, choose White as the new background, download the PNG, then open it in a passport photo maker to crop it to the right size.
