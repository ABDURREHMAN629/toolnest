What Week of the Year Is It? Week Numbers Explained (2026 Calendar)

By Abdur RehmanNovember 1, 20264 min read

Quick answer: the year is split into 52 or 53 numbered weeks. Most of the world uses the ISO 8601 system, where weeks start on Monday and week 1 is the week with the year's first Thursday. In the US, weeks usually start on Sunday and week 1 is the week containing January 1. The tool below shows today's week in both systems.

Week numbers are everywhere once you start looking: project plans, school terms, shipping schedules, payroll calendars and factory production plans all talk about "week 23" or "W45". The catch is that there are two common ways to count, and they don't always agree.

Because of these rules, the same date can be week 44 in one system and week 45 in the other. If you're coordinating with a team in another country, say which system you mean — or quote the dates.

Why some years have 53 weeks

A year has 365 days — 52 weeks plus one day (two in a leap year). Those spare days add up, so every five or six years the ISO calendar needs a 53rd week to stay in step. An ISO year gets 53 weeks when it starts on a Thursday, or when it's a leap year that starts on a Wednesday. 2020 and 2026 are 53-week years; the next one after 2026 is 2032.

This matters for anything paid or planned weekly. A business that pays weekly wages, or reports "weekly sales vs the same week last year", has to decide what to do with week 53 — usually it's compared with week 52 or reported separately.

Week numbers in Excel, Google Sheets and Outlook

  • Excel and Google Sheets: =ISOWEEKNUM(A1) gives the ISO week. =WEEKNUM(A1) gives the US-style week (Sunday start). =WEEKNUM(A1,2) starts weeks on Monday but still treats the week with January 1 as week 1, so it can differ from ISO.
  • Outlook: File → Options → Calendar → tick "Show week numbers in the month view and in the Date Navigator". Set "First week of year" to "First 4-day week" to match ISO.
  • Google Calendar: Settings → View options → "Show week numbers". It follows the first-day-of-week setting you choose.
  • iPhone: Settings → Calendar → turn on "Week Numbers".

How to write a week number

The ISO standard writes week dates as 2026-W45 (year, then W and the two-digit week) or 2026-W45-1 to add the weekday (1 = Monday, 7 = Sunday). In conversation, "week 45" or "W45" is fine. Watch out around New Year: December 29, 2025 belongs to 2026-W01, so the "week-year" isn't always the calendar year.

Frequently asked questions

How many weeks are in 2026?

Under the ISO 8601 system used in Europe and most business software, 2026 has 53 weeks, because it starts on a Thursday. Week 53 runs from Monday, December 28, 2026 to Sunday, January 3, 2027.

What week number is it today?

Open the week number tool on this page — it shows today's week automatically in both the ISO (Monday start) and US (Sunday start) systems, because the two can differ by one.

Does the week start on Sunday or Monday?

It depends on the system. ISO 8601 weeks start on Monday and are standard in Europe, the UK and most of Asia. In the US, calendars usually start the week on Sunday.

What is week 1 of the year?

In ISO 8601, week 1 is the week that contains the first Thursday of January (equivalently, the week with January 4 in it). In the US system, week 1 is simply the week that contains January 1.

How do I show week numbers in Excel?

Use =ISOWEEKNUM(A1) for ISO weeks or =WEEKNUM(A1) for US-style weeks that start on Sunday. In Google Sheets the same two functions work.
