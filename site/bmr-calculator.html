BMR calculator — your basal metabolic rate

Your BMR is the number of calories your body burns at complete rest just to stay alive — breathing, circulation, temperature. This bmr calculator uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, and if you enter your body fat percentage it also uses Katch-McArdle, which is more accurate for lean or muscular people — a bmr calculator with body fat (or bmr calculator with body fat percentage) option.

BMR calculator formula

The bmr calculator formula (Mifflin-St Jeor): men = 10 × weight(kg) + 6.25 × height(cm) − 5 × age + 5; women use −161 instead of +5. Example: a 30-year-old man, 80 kg, 180 cm → 1,780 kcal a day. As a bmr calculator for men (or bmr calculator for men with body fat) and a bmr calculator for women (a bmr calculator for women free of sign-ups), the formulas differ only by that constant.

Using BMR to lose weight

BMR isn't your daily calorie need — multiply it by your activity level, or use the Calorie Calculator. As a bmr calculator to lose weight (a bmr calculator for weight loss, or bmr calculator for women to lose weight), aim to eat a little below your total daily needs but not below your BMR for long periods.

Teens are still growing, so a bmr calculator for teens (or bmr calculator for 13 year old) gives only a rough figure, and it isn't intended as a bmr calculator for kids. These are the same equations the bmr calculator mayo clinic and other health sites reference.

For general information only — not medical advice. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian about your personal needs.
