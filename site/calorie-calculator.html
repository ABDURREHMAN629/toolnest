About this tool

Find how many calories you need per day to maintain, lose or gain weight based on your age, size and activity level. Calorie Calculator is part of WebToolNest's collection of free health & fitness. It works instantly in your browser with no account and no installation.

Calorie calculator for weight loss, maintenance and gain

Enter your age, sex, height, weight and activity level, then choose a goal. The calculator estimates your daily calories using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation (the most accurate standard formula for most adults) multiplied by your activity level, then adjusts for your goal.

Calorie calculator to lose weight

As a calorie calculator for weight loss (a calorie calculator to lose weight), it subtracts about 500 calories a day from maintenance — roughly 0.5 kg (1 lb) a week. Larger cuts are hard to sustain and can cost muscle. A calorie calculator for women gives lower numbers than for men of the same size because of body composition.

Calorie calculator to gain weight

As a calorie calculator to gain weight (a calorie calculator for weight gain) or calorie calculator for muscle gain, add about 250–500 calories a day and pair it with strength training and enough protein (around 1.6 g per kg of body weight).

Food, meals and recipes

This tool tells you how many calories you need. A calorie calculator food lookup (a calorie calculator for food, calorie calculator for meal or calorie calculator for recipes) tells you what you eat — read nutrition labels or use a food database, then compare with your target. One food calorie (kcal) = 4,184 joules (the familiar “1 calorie = 4.18400 joules” refers to the small calorie).

Not designed as a calorie calculator for kids — children's needs depend on growth, so ask a paediatrician — or as a calorie calculator for dogs or calorie calculator for cats (ask your vet).
