How CGPA to percentage conversion works

There is no single worldwide formula — the right one depends on your grading scale and, for 10-point systems, on your board or university. This converter supports the common ones:

  • CGPA × 9.5 — used by CBSE and most Indian universities. A CGPA of 8.2 becomes 8.2 × 9.5 = 77.9%.
  • CGPA × 10 — used by some universities that map the 10-point scale directly. 8.2 becomes 82%.
  • (CGPA − 0.75) × 10 — the VTU formula. 7.5 becomes (7.5 − 0.75) × 10 = 67.5%.
  • 4.0 and 5.0 scales — a simple proportion: CGPA ÷ maximum × 100. A 3.5 on a 4.0 scale is 87.5%.

Percentage to CGPA

The reverse conversion divides by the same factor. On the CBSE formula, 76% ÷ 9.5 = 8.0 CGPA. On a 4.0 scale, 80% is 0.80 × 4 = 3.2.

Which formula should I use?

Always check your transcript or university website first — many print the official formula on the back of the marksheet. If you are applying abroad, admissions offices usually ask for the conversion your own institution uses, or for a credential evaluation (WES, ECE) rather than a self-calculated figure.
