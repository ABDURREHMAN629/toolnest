About this tool

Flip a virtual coin online for heads or tails, and keep track of your results. Flip a Coin is part of WebToolNest's collection of free everyday utilities. It works instantly in your browser with no account and no installation.

Flip a coin — heads or tails

Click the coin or Flip coin to toss it with a 3D spin. You'll see Heads! or Tails!, a running count with percentages, and your last 40 results. Flip 10 times tosses ten at once. It's a flip a coin generator that's fair, free and instant — flip a coin heads or tails (or flip a coin for heads or tails) in one click.

Flip a coin for me

Need a quick decision? flip a coin for me — or flip a coin for me please — and let chance decide. Assign each side before you flip: it's a flip a coin for yes or no (flip a coin yes or no) answer, or a flip a coin for two options tie-breaker, like which film to watch. It works as a flip a coin for me heads or tails and flip a coin for me online tool — flip a coin please, and it's done.

Best of 3, or 100 times

  • flip a coin 3 times (flip a coin for me 3 times) — best of three settles arguments fairly; the count keeps score.
  • flip a coin 100 times — press “Flip 10 times” ten times and watch the percentages settle near 50/50 (the law of large numbers in action).

For two people

To flip a coin for us — two people deciding who goes first or who pays — agree on who is heads before flipping. Like the flip a coin google (flip a coin for me google) answer, it's instant, but this one keeps a tally and needs no flip a coin app. A simple flip a coin.

Results use your browser's cryptographically secure random generator, so every outcome is equally likely.
