Compound interest calculator formula

The compound interest calculator formula is A = P(1 + r/n)ⁿᵗ. Example: $10,000 at 8% compounded monthly for 10 years grows to about $22,196 — more than doubling with no extra deposits. Add $200 a month and it reaches roughly $58,800. That's the same calculation the SEC's compound interest calculator investor.gov uses.
