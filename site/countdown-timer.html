About this tool

Set an online countdown timer with an alarm sound. Use quick presets or set any duration. Countdown Timer is part of WebToolNest's collection of free date & time. It works instantly in your browser with no account and no installation.

Countdown timer online with alarm

Click a preset — 1, 3, 5, 10, 15, 20, 30 or 45 minutes, or 1 hour — or set any hours, minutes and seconds, then press Start. A progress bar shows the time left, the browser tab shows the remaining time too, and when it reaches zero an alarm rings until you stop it. It's a countdown timer online that's completely countdown timer free, with a full-screen button (⛶).
