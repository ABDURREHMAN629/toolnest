Day of the week calculator formula

The classic day of the week calculator formula is Zeller's congruence: h = (q + ⌊13(m+1)/5⌋ + K + ⌊K/4⌋ + ⌊J/4⌋ + 5J) mod 7, where q is the day, m the month (March = 3 … February = 14 of the previous year), K the year of the century and J the century; h = 0 is Saturday. It's the day of the week calculator for any date formula taught in maths classes. It's also the usual day finder formula. For mental day of the week calculator math, try John Conway's Doomsday rule — 4/4, 6/6, 8/8, 10/10 and 12/12 always fall on the same weekday in any year.

In Excel

For an excel day of week calculator (what day of the week formula excel), use =TEXT(A2,"dddd") for the name or =WEEKDAY(A2) for a number (1 = Sunday).

Julian calendar and BC dates

Dates before 1582 are calculated on the modern Gregorian calendar extended backwards. Historians usually quote older dates on the Julian calendar, so a day of the week calculator julian calendar answer can differ — convert the date to Gregorian first. Browser date pickers start at year 1, so a day of the week calculator bc date isn't supported.
