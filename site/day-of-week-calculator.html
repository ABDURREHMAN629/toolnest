How to use the Day of the Week Calculator

  1. Enter any date, past or future
  2. See the day of the week instantly
  3. Check the week number, day of the year and how long ago it was

Which weekday is any date?

Enter any date, past or future, to see the day of the week. You also get its place in the month (“the third Saturday”), the day of the year, the ISO week number, and how many days ago or away it is.

What day were you born?

Type your date of birth — 15 January 2000, for example, was a Saturday — and you'll also see the line from the old nursery rhyme for your day (“Saturday's child works hard for a living”). Try your birthday in a future year to see which day your next party falls on.

Famous dates

  • 4 July 1776, the US Declaration of Independence — a Thursday.
  • 6 June 1944, D-Day — a Tuesday.
  • 20 July 1969, the Moon landing — a Sunday.
  • 11 September 2001 — a Tuesday.
  • In 2026, 4 July and Halloween both fall on a Saturday, and Christmas Day on a Friday.

Working it out by hand

The classic formula is Zeller's congruence: h = (q + ⌊13(m+1)/5⌋ + K + ⌊K/4⌋ + ⌊J/4⌋ + 5J) mod 7, where q is the day, m the month (March = 3 … February = 14 of the previous year), K the year of the century and J the century; h = 0 is Saturday. For mental arithmetic, try John Conway's Doomsday rule: 4/4, 6/6, 8/8, 10/10 and 12/12 always fall on the same weekday in any year.

In Excel

Use =TEXT(A2,"dddd") for the day's name or =WEEKDAY(A2) for a number (1 = Sunday).

Old dates

Dates before 1582 are calculated on the modern Gregorian calendar extended backwards. Historians usually quote older dates on the Julian calendar, so convert a historical date to Gregorian first. Browser date pickers start at year 1, so dates BC aren't supported.
