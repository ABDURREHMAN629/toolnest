How fraction maths works

  • Add / subtract — find a common denominator: 1/3 + 1/4 = 4/12 + 3/12 = 7/12.
  • Multiply — multiply across: 2/3 × 3/5 = 6/15 = 2/5.
  • Divide (fraction calculator division) — flip the second fraction and multiply: 3/4 ÷ 1/2 = 3/4 × 2 = 1 ½.
  • Simplify (fraction calculator simplify) — divide top and bottom by their greatest common divisor.

That's the whole fraction calculator formula. Negative fractions work too — a fraction calculator with negatives.
