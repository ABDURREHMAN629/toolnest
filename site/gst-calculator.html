GST and VAT calculator — add or remove tax

This gst calculator and vat calculator works both ways. Choose Add tax to get the price including tax, or Remove tax to split a tax-inclusive price into its net amount and tax — a reverse gst calculator. Ready-made rates include 5% (GST India / VAT UAE), 12%, 18% and 28% (GST India), 15% (VAT Saudi Arabia), 17% and 18% (GST Pakistan) and 20% (VAT UK), plus any custom rate.

GST calculator formula

  • Add tax: total = amount × (1 + rate). 1,000 + 18% = 1,180.
  • Remove tax (the gst inclusive formula and reverse gst calculator formula): net = total ÷ (1 + rate). 1,180 ÷ 1.18 = 1,000, so the tax is 180. A common mistake is taking 18% of 1,180 (212.40) — that's wrong.

That's the whole gst calculator formula.
