LCM and GCD (HCF) calculator

Enter two or more numbers, separated by commas or spaces, to get both the LCM (least common multiple) and the GCD — also called the HCF or GCF (greatest common factor). It's an lcm calculator, a gcd calculator and an hcf calculator in one — an lcm gcd calculator and lcm gcf calculator for any count of numbers.

Worked examples

  • what is the lcm of 24 and 36? 24 = 2³ × 3 and 36 = 2² × 3², so the LCM takes the highest powers: 2³ × 3² = 72; the GCD takes the lowest: 2² × 3 = 12.
  • what is the lcm of 8 24 and 36? 2³ × 3² = 72.
  • Shortcut for two numbers: LCM = a × b ÷ GCD → 24 × 36 ÷ 12 = 72.

Methods you'll see in class

  • hcf calculator prime factorization (an hcf calculator with steps prime factorization approach) — break each number into primes, as above.
  • hcf calculator by division method (an hcf calculator with steps division method) — the gcd calculator euclidean algorithm: divide, keep the remainder, repeat. 36 ÷ 24 = 1 r 12; 24 ÷ 12 = 2 r 0 → GCD 12.
  • lcm calculator ladder method — divide all numbers by common primes in a ladder, then multiply the side and bottom numbers.
