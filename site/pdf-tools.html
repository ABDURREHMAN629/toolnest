PDF Tools

Merge, split, compress, convert, sign and edit PDF files online for free. Everything runs in your browser, so your documents are never uploaded.

🔗 Merge PDFCombine multiple PDF files into one document. Reorder the files before merging and download a single PDF — free, fast and private. ✂️ Split PDFSplit a PDF into single pages or extract custom page ranges such as 1-3, 5, 8-10 into separate PDF files. 🗜️ Compress PDFReduce PDF file size to meet email and upload limits. Pick a compression level and compare the before and after size. 🖼️ PDF to JPGConvert every page of a PDF into high-quality JPG or PNG images. Download single pages or all pages as a ZIP. 🖼️ PDF to PNGConvert PDF pages to sharp, lossless PNG images online. Free, no sign-up and your file never leaves your device. 📑 JPG to PDFTurn JPG, PNG, WebP and other images into one PDF. Choose page size, orientation and margins. 📝 PDF to WordConvert PDF documents into editable Word (DOCX) files. Text and paragraphs are extracted so you can edit them in Word or Google Docs. 📄 Word to PDFConvert Microsoft Word DOCX documents to PDF while keeping fonts, headings, tables and images. 🔄 Rotate PDFRotate all pages or selected pages of a PDF by 90, 180 or 270 degrees and save the result permanently. 🗂️ Organize PDFReorder, rotate and delete PDF pages visually using page thumbnails, then save a new PDF. 🗑️ Delete PDF PagesRemove unwanted pages from a PDF by entering page numbers or ranges, and download the cleaned file. #️⃣ Add Page Numbers to PDFInsert page numbers into a PDF. Choose the position, format (1, Page 1, 1 of N), font size and starting number. 💧 Watermark PDFStamp a text watermark such as CONFIDENTIAL or DRAFT on every page of a PDF with your own opacity, size, color and angle. 🔤 PDF to TextExtract all the text from a PDF file. Copy it to the clipboard or download it as a TXT file. ✒️ Sign PDFAdd your signature to a PDF online. Draw or type a signature, place it on any page and download the signed document.
