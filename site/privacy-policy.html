Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-21

Files and data you use in our tools

Our tools are designed to run inside your web browser. Files you select (PDFs, images, videos, audio, documents) and text you type are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers. Some features download helper components (for example the video engine, OCR language data or the background-removal AI model) from public content delivery networks; these downloads do not include your files.

Tools that use online services

A few tools must contact a third-party service to work: the currency converter (exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API), What Is My IP (IP lookup from ipwho.is), the internet speed test (Cloudflare speed test servers) and the YouTube thumbnail downloader (YouTube image servers). Those services receive your IP address as part of the normal web request.

Local storage

Some tools save your preferences or content (such as notes, to-do items or theme choice) in your browser's local storage so they are there next time. This data stays on your device and you can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Analytics and advertising

We may use analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to understand how the site is used and advertising services (such as Google AdSense) to keep the tools free. These providers may use cookies to serve and measure ads. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings.
