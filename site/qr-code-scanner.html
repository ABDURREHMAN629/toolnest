About this tool

Scan QR codes with your camera or from an image file, right in your browser. QR Code Scanner is part of WebToolNest's collection of free everyday utilities. It works instantly in your browser with no account and no installation.

QR code scanner online

Click Scan with camera and point your camera at a QR code — it's read instantly with a beep. No camera handy? Drop a screenshot or photo, choose a file, or paste an image with Ctrl + V. You can copy the result, open links (with a warning to check them first, as QR codes can lead to scam sites), and see the network name of Wi-Fi QR codes. A history lists everything you scanned this session. It's a qr code scanner online and qr code scanner free to use — a qr code scanner for free with nothing to install, and decoding happens on your device.

QR code scanner from image

As a qr code scanner from image (or qr code scanner for pictures), it's perfect when the QR code is already on your screen — in an email, a PDF or a website — where you can't point a camera at it. Take a screenshot (Win + Shift + S on Windows, Cmd + Shift + 4 on a Mac) and paste it here.

On PC, laptop and Mac

As a qr code scanner for pc or qr code scanner for computer, use a screenshot as above, or hold a printed code up to your webcam. It works as a qr code scanner for laptop with a built-in camera, and as a qr code scanner for mac (or qr code scanner for macbook) in Safari or Chrome.

On Android and iPhone

As a qr code scanner online mobile, open this page and allow the camera — it uses the rear camera. On an iPhone (a qr code scanner for iphone), the built-in Camera app also reads QR codes; on Android (a qr code scanner android, qr code scanner for android or qr code scanner for android phone), the camera or Google Lens does — the qr code scanner google option. This page is handy when those aren't available, or for scanning images, without another qr code scanner app.

It reads QR codes, not regular barcodes. To make your own code, use the QR Code Generator. A simple qr code scanner.
