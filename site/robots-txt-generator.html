What is a robots.txt file?

what is a robots.txt file? A plain text file at the root of your site that tells crawlers which pages they may visit — the robots.txt file meaning in one line. how robots.txt works — robots.txt explained: before crawling, a bot reads yoursite.com/robots.txt and follows the rules for its user-agent. The robots.txt file location must be the root; it won't work in a sub-folder.

Robots.txt example

A robots.txt example (robots.txt file example) in the standard robots.txt format:

User-agent: *
Disallow: /admin/
Allow: /admin/help/

Sitemap: https://example.com/sitemap.xml

Two robots.txt rules to know: robots.txt disallow all (or robots.txt to disallow all, robots.txt to block all crawlers) is Disallow: /, and an empty Disallow: allows everything. Google ignores Crawl-delay; Bing respects it.

Robots.txt for AI crawlers

As a robots.txt for ai (robots.txt for ai crawlers, robots.txt disallow ai or robots.txt ai bots) setup, the AI option adds a Disallow: / group for each AI crawler. Be aware it also blocks AI search bots such as OAI-SearchBot and PerplexityBot, so your pages may stop appearing in AI answers — remove those lines if you want that traffic.

Is robots.txt necessary?

is robots.txt necessary? Not strictly — without one, everything may be crawled — but it's good practice for the sitemap line. It isn't security: is robots.txt legally binding? No, it's a request well-behaved bots follow. To keep a page out of Google, use a noindex tag instead. robots.txt for seo: never block CSS, JS or pages you want ranked. Test it with a robots.txt tester or robots.txt checker such as Search Console's robots.txt report.
