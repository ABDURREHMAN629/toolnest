About this tool

Convert pay between hourly, daily, weekly, monthly and yearly salary. Salary Calculator is part of WebToolNest's collection of free finance calculators. It works instantly in your browser with no account and no installation.

Salary calculator — hourly, weekly, monthly and yearly

Enter what you're paid and how often — per hour, day, week, 2 weeks, month or year — plus your hours per week, days per week and any unpaid weeks off. The calculator converts it into every other period. It's a salary calculator hourly rate converter and a salary calculator for hourly rate questions such as “what is $25 an hour per year?” (about $52,000 at 40 hours × 52 weeks).

Before tax

The results are gross pay (before tax). Take-home pay depends on federal, state and local taxes: a salary calculator texas (or salary calculator for texas) and salary calculator florida (a salary calculator for florida) result has no state income tax, while a salary calculator california (a salary calculator for california), salary calculator new york or salary calculator nyc estimate does, as do a salary calculator illinois, salary calculator georgia, salary calculator north carolina and a salary calculator for maryland.

Other uses

  • salary calculator for raises — compare your current and new pay in every period.
  • salary calculator for teachers and others paid for fewer weeks — add the unpaid weeks.
  • salary calculator for mortgage planning — lenders often like housing costs under 28% of gross monthly income.
  • salary calculator for different cities and a salary calculator for inflation — compare offers knowing living costs and prices differ.
