Simple interest calculator formula

The simple interest calculator formula is SI = P × R × T ÷ 100. Example: 50,000 at 10% a year for 3 years → 50,000 × 10 × 3 ÷ 100 = 15,000 interest, 65,000 in total. Unlike compound interest, the interest is never added to the principal, so it grows in a straight line.
