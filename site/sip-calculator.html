SIP calculator formula

The sip calculator formula is FV = P × [((1 + i)ⁿ − 1) ÷ i] × (1 + i), where i is the monthly return and n the number of months. Example: 5,000 a month at 12% for 10 years → about 11.6 lakh (1,161,695), from 6 lakh invested.

Long-term SIPs

Compounding rewards patience. Compare a sip calculator for 10 years, a sip calculator for 20 years and a sip calculator for 50 years — the last decade usually adds more than the first two combined.
