How to use the Sleep Calculator

  1. Choose "wake up at" or "go to bed at"
  2. Pick the time
  3. See the best times based on sleep cycles

Wake up at the end of a sleep cycle

Sleep runs in cycles of about 90 minutes, and waking at the end of a cycle feels much easier than waking in the middle of deep sleep. Choose Wake up at… to find good bedtimes, Go to bed at… to find good wake-up times, or Sleep now. Adjust the time to fall asleep — about 15 minutes for most people.

How much sleep do you need?

  • Adults: 7–9 hours (5–6 cycles). Needs are similar for men and women.
  • Teenagers: 8–10 hours — aim for 6 cycles.
  • Children aged 6–12: 9–12 hours; a fixed bedtime helps most.
  • Toddlers (11–14 hours including naps) and babies (12–16 hours) sleep in shorter cycles, so treat the calculator as a rough guide only.

These ranges follow the American Academy of Sleep Medicine's recommendations by age.

Naps and travel

  • Naps: 20 minutes for a quick boost, or a full 90-minute cycle. Avoid 45–60 minutes, which tends to end in deep sleep.
  • Jet lag: shift your bedtime by an hour a day before you fly, and get morning daylight at your destination.

An example: waking up at 7:00

With the default 15 minutes to fall asleep, the calculator counts back in 90-minute cycles from 7:00 AM:

  • 6 cycles (9 hours of sleep): go to bed at 9:45 PM
  • 5 cycles (7.5 hours): go to bed at 11:15 PM
  • 4 cycles (6 hours): go to bed at 12:45 AM
  • 3 cycles (4.5 hours): go to bed at 2:15 AM, only if you have no other choice

Frequently asked questions

What time should I go to bed to wake up at 6 AM?

Around 10:15 PM for five full cycles (7.5 hours), or 8:45 PM for six cycles (9 hours). Both include about 15 minutes to fall asleep.

Is a sleep cycle always 90 minutes?

No. 90 minutes is an average. Cycles range from about 70 to 120 minutes and tend to get longer later in the night, so treat the times as a starting point.

Why does the calculator add time to fall asleep?

Most people take 10–20 minutes to fall asleep. Adding it means the cycles start when you actually drift off, not when you get into bed. You can change it to match yourself.
