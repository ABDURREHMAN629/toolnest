About this tool

Convert time between time zones and see the current time in cities around the world. Time Zone Converter is part of WebToolNest's collection of free date & time. It works instantly in your browser with no account and no installation.

Time zone converter with date

Pick a date and time and the time zone it's in, and see the same moment in every city on your list — with each place's date, UTC offset and a ☀️/🌙 day-or-night icon. Add any city or time zone from the full world list, remove the ones you don't need, and press Now for the current time everywhere. Your list is remembered on this device. Daylight saving time is applied automatically for the date you choose, so this time zone converter with date stays correct all year.

Example

A 9:00 AM meeting in New York is 7:30 PM in India in winter (EST, UTC−5) but 6:30 PM in summer (EDT, UTC−4) — because India (UTC+5:30) doesn't use daylight saving. That's why a time zone converter for india and a time zone converter for usa (Eastern, Central, Mountain, Pacific) need the date.

Meetings across countries

As a time zone converter for meetings (a time zone converter meeting planner), add every attendee's city and slide the time until it's daytime ☀️ for everyone — a time zone converter for multiple countries at a glance, like a time zone converter chart. It's a time zone calculator for travel too: check the local time when you land.

Forex market hours

As a time zone converter forex traders use, convert the session opens to your time. In time zone converter forex market hours terms (UTC, winter): Sydney about 21:00–06:00, Tokyo 00:00–09:00, London 08:00–17:00, New York 13:00–22:00 — about an hour earlier when daylight saving is on.
