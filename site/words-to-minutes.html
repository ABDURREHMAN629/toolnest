How long is a 1000 word speech?

how long is a 1000 word speech? At an average 130 wpm, about 7 minutes 42 seconds — between 6¼ minutes (fast) and 10 minutes (slow). how many minutes is a 1000 word speech to read silently? About 4 minutes. And how long is a 700 word speech? About 5 minutes 23 seconds.

How many words is a 5 minute speech?

how many words is a 5 minute speech? About 650 words at an average pace (500–800 depending on speed). Likewise, how many words for a 5 minute presentation: 650; how many words for a 10 minute speech: about 1,300. For how many words in a 5 minute speech toastmasters clubs expect: a 5–7 minute speech is roughly 650–900 words.

Words per minute speech averages

The words per minute speech average is 120–150 for presentations and up to 160 in conversation. The average words per minute for speech making is lower when you pause for effect — a good thing. As words per minute for presentation advice, aim for 120–140, and practise out loud with a timer: your real pace is the best number to enter here. Silent reading is faster, so a words to minutes reading time estimate is shown separately.
